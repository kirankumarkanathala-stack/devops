Why cloud?

Cloud means renting computers by the minute instead of buying and running your own.

Take Swiggy. Their job is delivering food, not managing servers, air conditioning, generators and guards. So they rent capacity from AWS and pay only for what they use. Even banks are now moving to the cloud.

Own servers (on-premise)AWS cloud
CostBig upfront purchase (CAPEX)Pay per minute of use
Space & powerYou need a room, cooling, generators, securityAWS handles all of it
Setup timeWeeks to monthsA new server in about 2 minutes
Staying onlineYou must build the backup yourselfEasy to spread across several locations

How AWS is laid out

AWS is split into regions, and each region is split into availability zones.

Region: Hyderabad (ap-south-2)

Availability Zone BIts own data center, power and cooling
Data center
A building full of servers, power, cooling and network cables.
Availability Zone (AZ)
One or more data centers with their own power, cooling and network, so one AZ failing doesn't take down another.
Region
A place on the map (N. Virginia, Mumbai, Hyderabad) with at least two AZs, usually three or more.

Why zones matter

A flood or power failure can knock out one data center. If your app runs in two AZs, traffic shifts to the healthy one and users notice nothing. This is high availability.

Which region for learning?

The instructor suggests us-east-1 (N. Virginia): it's the cheapest, and new AWS services launch there first. It's a little farther from India, but a few extra milliseconds don't matter when you're just learning.

Before you sign up: use a credit or debit card with international payments switched on (local-only cards get rejected), and make sure your billing name and address match your bank records.

What is a computer?

A server is just a computer with a different job.

  • CPUThe brain. Does the work.
  • RAMShort-term memory for whatever is running now.
  • StorageLong-term memory. Files stay here.
  • OSThe manager that runs programs on the hardware.
  • IP addressIts address on the network.

Your laptop and a server have all of these. The only difference is purpose: a laptop is for you, while a server sits in a data center and serves other people's requests 24/7. Phones, smart TVs, voting machines and the Raspberry Pi are computers too.

Storage has shrunk dramatically: about 60 years ago a room-sized drum held 1 KB. Today a fingernail-sized microSD card holds 1 TB.

What Linux really is

Linux is a kernel, not a full operating system.

You and your apps
Shell takes your typed commands and passes them on
Kernel (this is Linux) controls the CPU, RAM and disk
Hardware

Linus Torvalds started it in 1991 as a free, open-source alternative to expensive Unix systems that were locked to specific hardware. He also created Git.

Distros: Linux packaged into a full OS

Debian family

Debian, Ubuntu. Popular on desktops and with startups.

Red Hat family

RHEL, CentOS, AlmaLinux, Fedora, Amazon Linux.

Free vs enterprise: with a free distro, when something breaks you search forums and wait. With RHEL, companies and banks pay Red Hat for 24/7 support, and engineers join a call right away when production goes down.

Security groups

A security group is a firewall around your EC2 server. It decides who can connect in.

  • Ingress (traffic coming in) is checked. Nothing gets in unless you allow it.
  • Egress (traffic going out) is allowed by default.

To log in over SSH you allow port 22. The important choice is who is allowed. Try both options:

Allow SSH (port 22) from…
Type: SSH Port: 22 Source: 0.0.0.0/0
Youyour own IP
Allowed in
Your serverEC2 instance
Strangerany other IP, maybe via VPN
Allowed in too

0.0.0.0/0 means every IP address on the internet. /32 means exactly one IP address, yours.

It really happens. In an earlier batch, SSH was left open to everyone. A stranger connected through a VPN, logged in to the instructor's live server and deleted work in the middle of class. Rule: for port 22, always choose My IP.

Client and server

The client asks. The server answers.

  • Browsing Facebook: your laptop is the client, Facebook's data center is the server.
  • Copying photos from your phone: the laptop is the client, the phone is the server.
  • Same idea in real life: a patient asks, the doctor answers.

The internet between them is mostly not wireless or satellite. Countries are joined by huge fiber cables on the sea floor that come ashore at places like Chennai and Kolkata.

A protocol is an agreed set of rules two computers follow to talk. Each one uses a numbered port:

SSH keys

A lock you can show to anyone, and a key you never show to anyone.

Ways to prove who you are, from weakest to strongest:

  1. Username + password easy to guess or copy
  2. Password + one-time code better, needs a second device
  3. SSH key pair most secure

Public key = the lock

File ends in .pub. It goes on the server. Anyone may see it.

Private key = the key

Stays on your laptop only. It's the only thing that opens the lock.

Golden rule: never share your private key with anyone. Not a friend, not your manager, not the company owner.

Try it: make and import a key

You'll do this once, and then reuse the key when you launch servers.

  1. Open a terminal. On Windows install and open Git Bash. On Mac or Linux, open the normal Terminal.

  2. Pick a folder. pwd shows where you are. cd moves you somewhere else.

    # where am I?
pwd
# go to a folder for your keys (Git Bash on Windows style path)
cd /c/DevOps/keys

  3. Create the key pair. Press Enter at the prompts to accept the defaults.

    ssh-keygen -f mykey

  4. Check what you got. Two files appear:

    • mykey.pub: the public key (the lock). Safe to upload.
    • mykey: the private key. Keep it secret.
    # print the public key so you can copy it
cat mykey.pub

  5. Import the public key into AWS. In the AWS Console go to EC2 → Key Pairs → Actions → Import key pair, then paste the contents of mykey.pub.

  6. Use it. When you launch an EC2 instance, pick this key pair. You can then log in over SSH without a password.

Why do companies use the cloud instead of their own servers?

No big upfront cost, no buildings or cooling to manage, servers in minutes, and you pay only for what you use.

What's the difference between a region and an availability zone?

A region is a place on the map. An availability zone is one or more separate data centers inside it. Using two zones keeps your app up if one fails.

Is Linux an operating system?

Not by itself. Linux is the kernel. Distros like Ubuntu and RHEL add tools around it to make a full OS.

What source should you set for SSH in a security group?

My IP (a /32 rule), never 0.0.0.0/0.

Which key goes to AWS, and which stays with you?

The public key (.pub) goes to AWS. The private key never leaves your machine.
